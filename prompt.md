# Website content guardrails

Use `src/lib/business.ts` as the source of business identity, domain and call/WhatsApp destinations. Use the existing Next.js stack and preserve the service and area route slugs.

Write for customers in Arlington County, Virginia. Lead with lockouts, vehicle access and commercial door problems, then residential key and hardware needs. Keep body text readable on small phones, use accessible contact controls and make the next step clear.

Call and WhatsApp are the service-request channels. Do not introduce forms or email contact routes. Do not invent reviews, response times, prices, credentials, insurance, warranties, hours, jobs or staff. A business name containing “24/7” is not independent verification of operating hours. Omit unsupported details from visible text and structured data.

Keep page content specific to its intent. Do not create neighborhood job stories or imply a walk-in office. Use self-referencing canonical URLs, matching visible FAQ/schema, internal links and the registry-based sitemap. Run the checks described in README.md before publishing.
